READ-ONLY / EACH ARM INDEPENDENTLY

Calibration inspector

Consistency is not physical validation. Saved ranges define conversion; live MIN/MAX are servo registers. Raw ticks already include the servo homing offset. Never apply it twice.

How to validate an arm without the other arm

Secure the arm and stop teleoperation. Inspect identity, units and saved/live warnings first. Use an independently measured joint angle relative to its parent link, not the rendered model or the other arm. A physical reference must follow the intended joint convention; do not assume an arbitrary straight pose is zero.

Capture at least three distinct, safe interior positions per joint, including a reference and a small positive movement. Check direction, scale and constant offset. Repeat in both directions to expose backlash. Do not force mechanical stops. Check gripper closed/intermediate/open separately: percent is fraction of its calibrated opening, not millimeters.

Move by hand only when safely supported and torque is already off. The leader is read-only here; this monitor never releases it automatically. Guided recalibration below can release torque.
